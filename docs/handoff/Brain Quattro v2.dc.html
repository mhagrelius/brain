Sourdough
Hydration is the whole game. Anything past 80% and the crumb opens up, but the dough stops holding a shape — see Bulk Fermentation before you push it, and log the result under #baking/notes.
What went wrong
The **starter** was cold, so the second rise never came. 21°C is the floor.
- Feed twice before a bake, not once.
- Watch the dough, not the clock.
- [x] buy rye flour
- [ ] proof at 26°C and measure
A slack dough is not a wet dough.
